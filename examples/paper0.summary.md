## Title
Soil Fungal Networks Under Drought

## Topics
soil fungal networks under drought; 16S and ITS sequencing

## Introduction
The paper examines soil fungal networks under drought.

## Methods
16s and its sequencing on 108 samples from grassland plots.

## Results
Fungal richness fell from 88.3 to 38.5 (p < 0.01).

## Discussion
Findings were robust to alternative models; confounders were measured.

## Conclusion
Fungal richness fell under the studied conditions.

## Relevance to research
Directly relevant: it is about drought and soil microbes, with field or sequencing data.
