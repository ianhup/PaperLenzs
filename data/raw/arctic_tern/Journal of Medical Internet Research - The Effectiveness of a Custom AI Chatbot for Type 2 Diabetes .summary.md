## Title
The Effectiveness of a Custom AI Chatbot for Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus Health Literacy: Development and Evaluation Study

## Topics
Retrieval-augmented generation; health literacy; chatbot evaluation; type 2 diabetes

## Introduction
Many patients seeking diabetes guidance online need accessible information that cites reliable sources.

## Methods
A custom retrieval-augmented chatbot is evaluated on 44 specialist-scored questions and a separate 16-query simulated consultation; answers are assessed for appropriateness and source attribution.

## Results
Thirty-two of 44 initial answers cited reference documents; 30 of those 32 were fully appropriate. All 16 simulated-consultation responses were rated fully appropriate and source-grounded.

## Discussion
Grounding and transparent sourcing improve interpretability, but the study evaluates diabetes education rather than health outcomes.

## Conclusion
A reference-grounded chatbot can support patient education when its source coverage is monitored.

## Relevance to research
Indirect methodological relevance: source-grounding and response evaluation may help a literature-summary tool distinguish evidence about Arctic terns from unsupported claims. The chatbot contains no tern or climate datasets.
