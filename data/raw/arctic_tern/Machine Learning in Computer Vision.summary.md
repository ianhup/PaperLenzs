## Title
Machine Learning in Computer Vision

## Topics
Machine learning; image classification; object detection; computer vision

## Introduction
The article surveys machine-learning approaches for extracting information and identifying objects in images and video.

## Methods
It reviews supervised, unsupervised, and semi-supervised strategies and describes an application using TensorFlow/Faster R-CNN Inception-V2 to detect cars and people.

## Results
The paper outlines common methods such as neural networks, support-vector machines, and clustering, along with a demonstration of image-based detection.

## Discussion
Computer-vision systems can automate visual measurement, but the reviewed applications are not ecological field validations.

## Conclusion
Machine-learning methods are useful for image recognition and associated automation tasks.

## Relevance to research
Indirect methodological relevance: computer vision could help identify Arctic terns or fish from field imagery, complementing GPS trackers. The paper itself contains no data on ocean warming or tern migratory routes.
