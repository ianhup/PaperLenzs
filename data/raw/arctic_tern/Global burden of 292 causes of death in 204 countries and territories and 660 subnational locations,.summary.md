## Title
Global burden of 292 causes of death in 204 countries and territories and 660 subnational locations, 1990–2023: a systematic analysis for the Global Burden of Disease Study 2023

## Topics
Global mortality; causes of death; epidemiology; population modeling

## Introduction
Estimating cause-specific mortality by location, sex, and age is essential for public-health comparisons and policy planning.

## Methods
GBD 2023 integrates tens of thousands of mortality sources and uses cause-of-death ensemble modeling to estimate 292 causes across 204 countries/territories and 660 subnational areas for 1990–2023.

## Results
The study documents shifting global mortality patterns; COVID-19 ranked first among several leading cause categories in 2021 but dropped substantially in global ranking by 2023.

## Discussion
Large harmonized models allow cross-location comparisons while remaining sensitive to data availability and classification uncertainty.

## Conclusion
The paper provides an updated systematic assessment of cause-specific human mortality.
