## Title
Problematic social media use among French psychology university students: Prevalence, correlates, and mediation–moderation analyses

## Topics
Problematic social media use; students; impulsivity; depression; survey

## Introduction
Intensive social media use may relate to mental health and self-regulation difficulties among university students.

## Methods
A cross-sectional online survey of 973 French psychology students uses the Bergen Social Media Addiction Scale, regression, and mediation/moderation analyses.

## Results
Prevalence depends strongly on classification: 15.0% using a descriptive threshold, 5.6% using a high-risk threshold, and 3.8% using functional impairment; impulsivity and depressive mood are among correlates.

## Discussion
Different thresholds produce very different prevalence estimates, and associations are not evidence of causation.

## Conclusion
Clear diagnostic criteria and careful attention to psychological correlates are needed.
