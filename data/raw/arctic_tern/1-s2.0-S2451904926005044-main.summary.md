## Title
Kriging and Neural Network models for pressure losses across perforated plates

## Topics
Kriging; neural networks; fluid dynamics; perforated plates; surrogate modeling

## Introduction
Pressure drops across perforated plates are often estimated by empirical formulas that do not generalize well.

## Methods
The paper trains Kriging and neural-network regressors on published experimental measurements, compares them with conventional correlations, and embeds the new models as momentum source terms in RANS channel-flow simulations.

## Results
Both data-driven models outperform many empirical formulas for the available plate configurations; computational-fluid-dynamics predictions are stable across the tested meshes.

## Discussion
Data scarcity limits the training domain despite promising predictive performance.

## Conclusion
Kriging and neural networks can be viable surrogate models for perforated-plate flow resistance.

## Relevance to research
Low methodological relevance: regression and validation strategies might help model how wind conditions relate to tern travel, but perforated-plate pressure loss is unrelated to migration biology or ocean food resources.
