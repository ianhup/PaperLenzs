## Title
Prevalence of sarcopenic obesity in a Danish population of adults with obesity

## Topics
Sarcopenic obesity; body composition; prevalence; Danish cohort

## Introduction
The frequency of low muscle strength and low muscle mass among Danish adults with obesity has been poorly characterized.

## Methods
Researchers evaluate screening data from 1,040 adults with obesity between 2020 and 2025 using grip strength, body composition, and consensus sarcopenic-obesity criteria.

## Results
Thirty-one participants met sarcopenic-obesity criteria, approximately 3.0% of the sample.

## Discussion
The study cannot establish a clear individual effect of sarcopenic obesity on associated metabolic diseases.

## Conclusion
Sarcopenic obesity appears uncommon in this relatively young Danish screening cohort.
