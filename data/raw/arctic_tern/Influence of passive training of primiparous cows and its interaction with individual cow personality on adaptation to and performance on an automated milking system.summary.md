## Title
Influence of passive training of primiparous cows and its interaction with individual cow personality on adaptation to and performance on an automated milking system

## Topics
Dairy cattle; automated milking; passive training; personality; adaptation

## Introduction
First-time users of robotic milking systems may require acclimation before adopting regular voluntary visits.

## Methods
Thirty-two first-lactation cows are assigned in pairs to 48-hour pre-exposure or control groups; personality tests and milking behavior are recorded through 21 days of robot use.

## Results
Pre-exposed cows averaged 6.8 versus 5.5 daily robot visits over days 2–20 and 33.5 versus 32.5 kg/day milk yield; boldness and activity affected some responses within the exposed group.

## Discussion
Early familiarity and individual behavioral differences influence adaptation, although sample size is small.

## Conclusion
Passive exposure before robotic milking can improve adjustment and production.
