## Title
Caught them all: Gaming disorder, motivations for playing and spending among core Pokémon Go players

## Topics
Mobile gaming; motivation; microtransactions; gaming disorder

## Introduction
The authors study why dedicated Pokémon Go players continue playing and spending and how problematic play is associated with motivation.

## Methods
Survey responses from 1,630 players at level 25 or higher are examined through motivational and gaming-disorder measures.

## Results
Relatedness and competence best predict play and spending; gaming-disorder classification varies by measure from about 1% to 9% and correlates with social anxiety and spending.

## Discussion
Self-reported, selected-player data limit interpretation for the general gaming population.

## Conclusion
Social motivation is central to behavior and monetization among committed players.
