## Title
Gaps in Glycaemic Monitoring and Underuse of Cardioprotective Glucose-lowering Agents: Challenges of inpatient diabetes care identified by EFIM’s Diabesity Day Survey 2024

## Topics
Inpatient diabetes; obesity; cardioprotective drugs; hospital practice

## Introduction
Diabetes and obesity commonly overlap among hospitalized adults, yet clinical monitoring and guideline-based care are inconsistent.

## Methods
A cross-sectional survey of records for 207 patients across eight European hospitals compares patients with and without diabesity, with logistic models for treatment choices.

## Results
Half of patients had diabesity; among those with type 2 diabetes, about a third lacked recent HbA1c data and only about 40% with cardio-renal disease received cardioprotective glucose-lowering therapy.

## Discussion
The study identifies quality-of-care gaps and associations with treatment decisions, but cross-sectional data cannot establish causes.

## Conclusion
Improved glucose monitoring and appropriate cardioprotective therapy may address inpatient care shortcomings.
