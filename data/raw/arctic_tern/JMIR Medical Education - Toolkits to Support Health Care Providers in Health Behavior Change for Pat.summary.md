## Title
Toolkits to Support Health Care Providers in Health Behavior Change for Patients With Chronic Conditions: Scoping Review

## Topics
Health behavior; clinical toolkits; implementation science; scoping review

## Introduction
Healthcare professionals need practical resources to support patients’ lifestyle changes, but effective toolkit-development processes are incompletely documented.

## Methods
A scoping review searches five databases through August 2026, includes 19 eligible studies, and synthesizes design processes, behavior-change theories, and implementation barriers.

## Results
Toolkit formats and development processes vary; 14 studies cite a theoretical model or framework. Time and practice context feature frequently among barriers.

## Discussion
End-user involvement and iterative development appear important, though the synthesis does not directly test a standardized toolkit.

## Conclusion
The review proposes a toolkit definition and design recommendations for future clinical interventions.

## Relevance to research
Not relevant: this is clinical implementation research, not an Arctic tern climate or migration study. Its evidence-synthesis procedures are general methods rather than subject evidence.
