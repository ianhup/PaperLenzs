## Title
Long-term impact of the Guxian Reservoir on water and sediment regulation in the middle reaches of the Yellow River Basin

## Topics
Reservoir operations; sediment transport; hydrological modeling; ecological flow

## Introduction
Sediment-heavy Yellow River flows require coordinated reservoir operations that balance water, hydropower, sediment, and ecology.

## Methods
The authors build a long-series multi-objective model of the Guxian, Sanmenxia, and Xiaolangdi reservoirs and simulate the first decade of Guxian operation under high-, normal-, and low-flow series.

## Results
The modeled Guxian reservoir improves July inflow to Xiaolangdi and sediment-management performance; a 15% sediment-release ratio is best under high flows, whereas 10% performs better in normal or low flows.

## Discussion
Hydrological conditions alter the preferable operating strategy; results are model-based projections for a river-reservoir system.

## Conclusion
Coordinated, flow-sensitive operation may improve environmental and engineering outcomes.

## Relevance to research
Not directly relevant: it analyzes freshwater reservoir sediment rather than seabirds. Its scenario-based environmental modeling could be a distant methodological analogy, but it cannot support claims about Arctic tern routes.
