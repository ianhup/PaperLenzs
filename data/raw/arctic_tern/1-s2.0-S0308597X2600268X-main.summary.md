## Title
Benchmarking sustainable blue economy in Canada and Norway: A comparative KPI-based assessment

## Topics
Blue economy; ocean governance; sustainability indicators; Canada; Norway

## Introduction
Canada and Norway have pursued differing approaches to environmentally sustainable ocean-based economies.

## Methods
Researchers assess 2015–2025 policy progress using a 20-indicator framework and a five-point comparative scale across blue growth, circularity, nature-based solutions, and carbon alignment.

## Results
Norway scores more strongly on ocean industrial development and shipping decarbonization; Canada shows relative strengths in marine conservation, restoration, and circular-economy governance.

## Discussion
Different institutional histories and policy priorities explain complementary strengths; indicator scoring is an indicative comparison, not a direct ecological experiment.

## Conclusion
Ocean sustainability strategies should be adapted to national institutions and capacities.

## Relevance to research
Indirect relevance: marine conservation and ocean decarbonization provide policy context for protecting Arctic tern feeding ecosystems. The paper does not measure tern migration, ocean temperature effects on fish, or bird survival.
