## Title
Teaching Human-Computer Interaction with the use of Generative Artificial Intelligence Design Tools and Prototypes

## Topics
Generative AI; human-computer interaction; education; prototyping; usability

## Introduction
The paper investigates how students use emerging generative-AI tools in a human-computer interaction course.

## Methods
Researchers compare student projects and tool-selection behaviors in 2025 and 2026, allow AI use for design and prototyping, and evaluate artifacts using usability measures including the System Usability Scale.

## Results
Students became more selective in how they used AI tools; evaluated AI-developed tools attained a mean usability score of 85.20, rated excellent.

## Discussion
Generative tools change student workflows, but the findings are course- and prototype-specific.

## Conclusion
The authors see AI design tools as potentially useful in HCI education when paired with evaluation.
