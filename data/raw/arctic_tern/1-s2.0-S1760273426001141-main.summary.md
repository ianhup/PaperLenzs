## Title
Multicenter, retrospective study of balloon valvuloplasty-associated pulmonary artery dissection in dogs with pulmonary valve stenosis

## Topics
Veterinary cardiology; balloon valvuloplasty; pulmonary artery dissection; survival

## Introduction
Pulmonary artery dissection is an uncommon complication of balloon treatment for pulmonary valve stenosis in dogs.

## Methods
A multicenter retrospective review examines records and images from 53 dogs that developed dissection during valvuloplasty, documenting intervention variables, diagnosis, and follow-up.

## Results
Forty-eight dogs (90.6%) survived discharge. Immediate angiography and transesophageal echocardiography detected dissection more often than transthoracic echocardiography.

## Discussion
Retrospective sampling prevents firm conclusions about the incidence or causal risk factors of dissection.

## Conclusion
Dogs affected by this complication can have favorable survival, and timely imaging is important.
