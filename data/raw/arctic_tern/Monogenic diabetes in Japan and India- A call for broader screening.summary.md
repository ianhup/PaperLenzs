## Title
Monogenic diabetes in Japan and India: A call for broader screening

## Topics
Monogenic diabetes; genetic screening; Japan; India; young-onset diabetes

## Introduction
Traditional clinical screening may fail to detect some monogenic diabetes cases among young people in Asia.

## Methods
The brief report analyzes findings from Japanese and Indian national cohorts, comparing clinical features and genetically identified cases against commonly used screening signals.

## Results
The studies find monogenic diabetes among young non-obese patients with varied presentations; up to roughly 70% of cases have a three-generation family history.

## Discussion
Reliance on strong family-history criteria alone may miss genetically treatable cases.

## Conclusion
The authors argue for more inclusive genetic testing criteria in Asian clinical practice.
