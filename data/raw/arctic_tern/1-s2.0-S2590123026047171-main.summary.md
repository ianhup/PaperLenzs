## Title
Physics-informed operator learning with dual-critic proximal policy optimization for time-sensitive PDE-constrained control: Application to river pollution management

## Topics
Environmental simulation; physics-informed learning; reinforcement learning; river pollution

## Introduction
Controlling time-varying river pollution requires fast predictive models that respect governing physical processes.

## Methods
A physics-informed Deep Operator Network surrogate is coupled to dual-critic proximal policy optimization; simulation tests cover accuracy, control ablations, and previously unseen regimes.

## Results
The surrogate attained 2.21% held-out relative L2 error. Compared with conventional PPO, the controller reduced integrated pollution exceedance by 11.65% and violation duration by 10.85%.

## Discussion
The evaluation shows gains within a synthetic advection–diffusion–reaction setting; field deployment is not established.

## Conclusion
Separating environmental reward from physics-consistency diagnostics can strengthen constrained control.

## Relevance to research
Indirect methodological relevance: its environmental modeling could inspire ocean-temperature or prey-distribution models, but its river-pollution controller does not investigate Arctic tern routes or explain the mock research findings.
