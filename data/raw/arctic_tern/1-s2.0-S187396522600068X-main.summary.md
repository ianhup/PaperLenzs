## Title
Forced liminality and the indigenous peoples of Siberia in the wake of the Russia–Ukraine war

## Topics
Indigenous peoples; Siberia; Russia–Ukraine war; migration; political identity

## Introduction
The war creates complex pressures on Siberian Indigenous communities, including difficult choices around political expression and cultural identity.

## Methods
The author uses previous studies, online materials, field notes, and multi-sited chronological reconstruction to compare experiences within Russia and among migrants abroad.

## Results
The analysis identifies diverse responses ranging from public state alignment and activism abroad to political silence and migration motivated by safety.

## Discussion
The concept of forced liminality helps interpret why silence need not reflect indifference but constrained choices.

## Conclusion
War-driven political conditions challenge self-determination and the preservation of Indigenous identities.
