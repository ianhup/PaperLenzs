## Title
Cost-effectiveness of advanced hybrid closed loop therapy compared to standard insulin therapy for type 1 diabetes in pregnancy: an economic evaluation of the CRISTAL trial

## Topics
Type 1 diabetes; pregnancy; insulin delivery; health economics

## Introduction
Automated insulin systems can improve maternal glycemic management but have higher equipment costs.

## Methods
A decision-tree economic model uses complication and hospitalization information from the CRISTAL trial over a 28-week pregnancy-to-discharge horizon, with scenario and sensitivity analyses.

## Results
Estimated costs were €13,988.75 for advanced hybrid closed-loop therapy versus €14,221.33 for standard insulin treatment, a modeled saving of €232.57 per patient.

## Discussion
Reduced acute-care use partly offsets device cost; uncertainty remains about longer-term pregnancy and offspring outcomes.

## Conclusion
The system may be cost-saving during pregnancy, pending broader supporting evidence.
