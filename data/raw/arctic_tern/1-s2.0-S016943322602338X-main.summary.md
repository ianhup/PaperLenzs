## Title
Surface modification of cellulose nanofibril films via organosilicon deposition using dielectric barrier discharge plasma (DBD) plasma for packaging applications

## Topics
Cellulose films; plasma coating; food packaging; moisture resistance

## Introduction
Biodegradable cellulose nanofibril packaging absorbs moisture, limiting its use as an alternative to petroleum plastics.

## Methods
The authors apply dielectric-barrier-discharge plasma using an organosilicon precursor and compare single surface depositions with multilayer coatings under altered gas and treatment conditions.

## Results
Optimized deposition made cellulose surfaces more water-repellent; mixed inorganic/organic multilayers further reduced water uptake relative to untreated films.

## Discussion
Surface chemistry, gas composition, and exposure settings determine moisture-barrier performance.

## Conclusion
Plasma surface modification offers a potential route to better bio-based packaging.
