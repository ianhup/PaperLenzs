## Title
“So you got the brain but have you got the touch?”: the impact of the social presence of service robot and service innovativeness on customer satisfaction

## Topics
Service robots; social presence; customer satisfaction; anthropomorphism

## Introduction
Service organizations use robots for functional tasks but must also consider customer perceptions during social interactions.

## Methods
Two online studies and one laboratory study investigate how robot social presence, perceived innovativeness, and anthropomorphism shape customer satisfaction.

## Results
Greater perceived robot social presence predicts satisfaction; service innovativeness mediates this effect for more innovative customers, while stronger anthropomorphism can weaken it.

## Discussion
Positive effects depend on customer and robot characteristics rather than simply maximizing human-like appearance.

## Conclusion
Service-robot design should balance social presence with an appropriate degree of anthropomorphism.
