## Title
Intra-arc tectonic deformation around the Irazú–Turrialba volcanic complex, Costa Rica

## Topics
Volcanic tectonics; seismicity; Costa Rica; earthquake relocation

## Introduction
The study investigates how tectonic plates and local structures deform crust near the Irazú and Turrialba volcanoes.

## Methods
A dedicated 42-station seismic network recorded earthquakes from April 2018 through March 2019; researchers relocated events, estimated a local seismic-velocity model, and assessed focal mechanisms.

## Results
The network located 967 local earthquakes and revealed distinct deformation patterns around the two volcanic centers.

## Discussion
The proposed model links Irazú more strongly to accumulated forearc-sliver motion and Turrialba to regional subduction.

## Conclusion
Seismic observations indicate that adjacent volcanoes may experience different tectonic regimes.

## Relevance to research
Not relevant to the Arctic tern project: it addresses crustal deformation and volcanic hazards, not global warming, ocean food availability, bird migration, or avian survival.
