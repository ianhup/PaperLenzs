## Title
Continuous assessment in medical education: Exploring students’ views on the progress test

## Topics
Progress testing in medical education

## Introduction
This study, which explored medical students’ perceptions of continuous assessment methods, including the PT, found that students generally viewed such methods positively, perceiving them as beneficial for their learning and professional development.

## Methods
Participants included medical students from both institutions who had completed at least one PT and volunteered to participate in the study.
