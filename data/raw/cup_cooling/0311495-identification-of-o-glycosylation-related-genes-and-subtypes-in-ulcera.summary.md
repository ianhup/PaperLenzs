## Title
Identification of O-glycosylation related genes and subtypes in ulcerative colitis based on machine learning

## Topics
Ulcerative colitis, gene expression and machine learning

## Introduction
In this study, bioinformatics methods were employed to explore the role of O-GlcNAcylation in developing ulcerative colitis.

## Methods
The following criteria from the Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO) database (www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/) retrieval of UC microarray datawere included: a) data from the same sequencing platform to generate expression of two different spectra; b) inclusion of human test samples only; and c) a minimum of ten samples per groups.

## Results
The results showed that 449 genes were co-up-regulated and 233 were co-down-regulated (Fig 1A).

## Discussion

## Conclusion
The analysis identified four genes associated with O-GlcNAcylation and used them to define two ulcerative colitis subtypes. The possible link between subtype B and colitis-associated colorectal cancer warrants further investigation.
