## Title
Supplemental online resources improve data literacy education: Evidence from a social science methods course

## Topics
Data literacy and supplemental learning resources

## Introduction
In this study, we introduce an OSI resource and show that it substantially improves data literacy education.

## Methods
Our sample consists of undergraduate students at UCSD who were enrolled in the course during Spring term of 2021, excluding those who opted out or did not take the midterm exam.

## Results
We find that access to the OSI modules significantly increased student performance.

## Discussion
Moreover, point estimates suggest that the benefits for student learning are greater the more deeply students engage with the OSI resources.

## Conclusion
In the randomized experiment, access to supplemental online modules improved performance on exam questions in the introductory data analysis course. The findings support using asynchronous modules to supplement data literacy instruction in that setting.
