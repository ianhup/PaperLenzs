## Discussion
This study also suggests that young adults’ continuation of education after marriage is associated with professional maternal healthcare utilization (≥4 professional ANC, ≥8 professional ANC, and professional delivery care services) with higher odds.

## Conclusion
This study is a novel contribution as it is the first to document the relationship between the continuation of education after marriage and the utilization of recommended professional antenatal care and professional delivery care among young adult women in Bangladesh.
