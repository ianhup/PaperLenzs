## Title
Unveiling multiscale spatiotemporal dynamics of volatility in high-frequency financial markets

## Topics
Financial volatility and time series

## Introduction
Investigating the effects of these big events is important and necessary.

## Methods
Specifically, we collected 5-min data of 225 stocks with the time period from 9:35 of 2016.1.4 to 15:00 of 2023.6.29.

## Results
The consistent power-law decay observed across all time scales suggests that the market dynamics have a universal characteristic in terms of volatility persistence.

## Discussion
The authors found stronger correlations among stocks and faster reactions to major events at finer time scales. The observed market relationships therefore depend in part on the interval used to measure volatility.

## Conclusion
Based on the 5-min high-frequency data of China’s financial institutions from 2016 to 2023, we have obtained the realized volatility for each stock for six time scales, i.e., 5-min, 10-min, 30-min, 1-hr, 2-hr and 4-hr.
