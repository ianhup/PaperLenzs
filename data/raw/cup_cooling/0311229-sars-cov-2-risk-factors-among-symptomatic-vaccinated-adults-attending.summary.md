## Title
SARS-CoV-2 risk factors among symptomatic vaccinated adults attending community testing locations in the Netherlands from June 2021 till February 2022

## Topics
COVID-19 risk in vaccinated adults

## Introduction
From a public health perspective it is important to keep investigating risk factors for a SARS-CoV-2 infection, because of the changing nature of the SARS-CoV-2 virus and the interventions to control it.

## Methods
For this study we used data from the CONTEST study, which was a test-negative case-control study among adults attending community SARS-CoV-2 testing facilities in the Netherlands from February 8 2021 till March 11 2022.
