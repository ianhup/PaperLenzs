## Title
Immunogenicity and safety of the MF59-adjuvanted seasonal influenza vaccine in non-elderly adults: A systematic review and meta-analysis

## Topics
Influenza vaccine immunogenicity and safety

## Introduction
The objective of this review was to systematically collect and evaluate available experimental and observational data on the immunogenicity, efficacy, effectiveness and safety of aTIV/aQIV in non-elderly adults.

## Methods
With regards to the last criterion, we included studies if the majority of participants were <65 years.

## Results
However, statistically significant differences were observed only in some, generally larger studies.

## Discussion
Some evidence suggests that aTIV/aQIV is also immunogenic against antigenically dissimilar strains, which may be indicative of a certain level of cross-protection.

## Conclusion
In non-elderly adults, MF59-adjuvanted influenza vaccines generally elicited stronger immune responses than standard-dose non-adjuvanted vaccines and had an acceptable safety profile in the reviewed studies. Evidence on efficacy and antibody persistence was limited and inconclusive.
