## Title
Automatic brake Driver Assistance System based on deep learning and fuzzy logic

## Topics
Driver assistance and automatic braking

## Introduction
This research is commonly motivated by evidence associated with human error in driving, aiming to develop systems that reduce human errors and driving risks to enhance safety in driving and autonomous vehicles, prioritizing efficiency, safety, and mobility optimization.

## Methods
A three-stage implementation of the ABDAST is proposed and implemented in a low-performance, low-power consumption embedded system.

## Results
The first experiment consists of evaluating EfficientDet D0 to detect and identify the state of traffic lights in a simplified version of the BSTLD.

## Discussion
The road test demonstrated that the prototype could combine traffic-light detection and distance estimates to trigger braking. Detection performance declined at the longer distance reported, so the prototype results should be read in the context of the tested distances and road conditions.

## Conclusion
Thus, the results suggest that EfficientDet D0 and deep learning models embedded in a Jetson TX2 have potential in this application.
