## Title
Expected features of the course leader in the rehabilitation healthcare professionals’ higher education: A qualitative study on students’ perspectives

## Topics
Rehabilitation education and course leadership

## Introduction
Therefore, the current qualitative study aimed to investigate the expected characteristics of a CL in rehabilitation healthcare professionals’ higher education from the perspective of students’ experiences.

## Methods
A qualitative interview study was carried out to explore students’ expected features of a course leader in a sample of Italian students or recent graduates at the Master of Science (MSc) degree in ‘Rehabilitation Sciences of Healthcare Professions’ (University of Verona, Verona, Italy).

## Results
Ten Italian students or recent graduates at the University of Verona agreed to partake in the study (Age (mean and deviation standard): 30 ± 9; 20% Men, N = 2; 80% Women, N = 8; all white Italian).

## Discussion
Not less important was the honesty and transparency during communication, including the ability to admit limitations, as our students and other authors highlighted as paramount characteristics [18, 21].

## Conclusion
The results of this study highlighted that students expect the CLs in the rehabilitation healthcare professionals’ higher education to have advanced soft skills (adaptability, communication, organisation, teamwork) and hard skills (i.e., clinical experience, evidence-based practice updated).
