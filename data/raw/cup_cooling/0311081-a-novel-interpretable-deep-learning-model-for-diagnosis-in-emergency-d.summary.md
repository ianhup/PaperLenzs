## Title
A novel interpretable deep learning model for diagnosis in emergency department dyspnoea patients based on complete data from an entire health care system

## Topics
Emergency diagnosis and interpretable deep learning

## Introduction
In this study, we aimed to create an AI diagnostic decision support for dyspnoeic adults at time of ED triage.

## Methods
Informed consent was waived and the participants were instead given an opt-out possibility in accordance with the ethical approval.

## Results
Among the 10,315 visits, the number of unique patients was 6,967.

## Discussion
The CatBoost models for each class may be less calibrated compared to the single CareNet model, explaining the lower average micro AUROC value for the CatBoost model.

## Conclusion
We developed an AI tool for diagnosing dyspnoeic adults at the time of triage in the ED by analysing comprehensive data from an entire regional healthcare system.
