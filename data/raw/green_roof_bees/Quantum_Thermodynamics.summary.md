## Title
Quantum Thermodynamics

## Topics
Quantum information; work and heat; fluctuation relations; thermalization; resource theory; thermal machines

## Introduction
Thermodynamics at small scales and away from equilibrium requires attention to fluctuations, coherence, and quantum correlations that are less prominent in conventional macroscopic descriptions.

## Methods
This overview synthesizes theoretical frameworks and selected experiments, covering information-thermodynamics links, work definitions, fluctuation theorems, quantum dynamics, single-shot resource theories, and quantum engines and refrigerators.

## Results
The review connects several approaches to work extraction, thermal operations, and information processing, while describing how quantum resources affect thermodynamic tasks. It does not present a single original experiment or one common numerical treatment effect.

## Discussion
Definitions of quantum work and operational relationships between ensemble, fluctuating, and single-shot descriptions remain incompletely unified. Strong coupling, thermalization, and genuinely quantum advantages pose continuing theoretical and experimental questions.

## Conclusion
Quantum thermodynamics has developed complementary tools, but clearer operational connections and testable predictions are needed to unify the field.
