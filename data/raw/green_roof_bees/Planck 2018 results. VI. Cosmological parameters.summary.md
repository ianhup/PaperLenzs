## Title
Planck 2018 results. VI. Cosmological parameters

## Topics
Cosmic microwave background; cosmological parameters; Lambda-CDM; polarization; lensing; Bayesian inference

## Introduction
Precise measurements of the cosmic microwave background constrain the Universe's composition and evolution. This paper reports cosmological inference from Planck's final full-mission measurements.

## Methods
Temperature and polarization data and lensing reconstruction were analyzed with likelihood models incorporating foregrounds, instrumental effects, and parameter uncertainties. The six-parameter spatially flat Lambda-CDM model and several extensions were assessed, with external datasets used in additional comparisons.

## Results
The combined data were consistent with base Lambda-CDM. Reported model-dependent estimates included Hubble constant 67.4 +/- 0.5 km/s/Mpc, matter-density parameter 0.315 +/- 0.007, and fluctuation amplitude sigma_8 of 0.811 +/- 0.006.

## Discussion
Some parameter estimates depend on the adopted cosmological model. The authors examined internal consistency, remaining systematic uncertainties, and tensions with certain external measurements; improved precision does not remove model dependence.

## Conclusion
The final Planck analysis tightly constrains standard cosmological parameters without compelling evidence requiring the studied model extensions.
