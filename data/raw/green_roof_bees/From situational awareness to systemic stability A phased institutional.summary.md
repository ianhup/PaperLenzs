## Title
From situational awareness to systemic stability: A phased institutional pathway for space traffic management

## Topics
Space governance; orbital debris; coordination; institutional design; interoperability; space traffic management

## Introduction
Increasing satellite traffic creates coordination demands that tracking information alone cannot resolve. The paper distinguishes awareness of orbital hazards from a predictable traffic-management regime.

## Methods
A conceptual institutional and legal analysis compares governance models from aviation, navigation, standards organizations, and maritime systems. It proposes an Orbital Stability Assessment, a periodic index, and staged development of a standard-setting Space Traffic Management Authority.

## Results
The proposed pathway moves from interoperable information and procedures toward institutional standard setting, distributed implementation, and hybrid compliance incentives. The assessment and index are proposed diagnostics of coordination burden, not validated measurements from an implemented pilot.

## Discussion
Participation incentives, free riding, geopolitical fragmentation, and verification constrain implementation. The framework prioritizes incremental convergence rather than immediate centralized operational control.

## Conclusion
Scalable space traffic management requires common expectations and institutions that convert situational awareness into predictable coordination.
