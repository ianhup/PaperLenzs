## Title
Current practices to reduce the risk of transmission of Clostridioides difficile in France: results of a national survey

## Topics
Infection prevention; hospital hygiene; contact precautions; sporicidal cleaning; national survey

## Introduction
Recommended measures can reduce opportunities for C. difficile transmission, but their implementation may differ among hospitals. This study describes reported French infection-control practices.

## Methods
An anonymous online survey conducted from August to December 2022 collected responses from healthcare professionals across France. Descriptive analyses summarized contact precautions, protective equipment, hand hygiene, and environmental cleaning.

## Results
Among 350 respondents, 70.3% belonged to infection-control teams. Seventy percent reported contact precautions for symptomatic patients, 49% continued them at least 48 hours after diarrhea resolution, and 71% reported cleaning with sporicidal products. Practices varied across measures.

## Discussion
Self-reported implementation does not establish actual compliance or measure reductions in infection transmission. The findings identify inconsistencies for further investigation rather than demonstrating comparative effectiveness.

## Conclusion
Core prevention practices are widely recognized but unevenly implemented, supporting more detailed evaluation and efforts to standardize practice.
