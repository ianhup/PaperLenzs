## Title
Supplementary information for 'Quantum supremacy using a programmable superconducting processor'

## Topics
Superconducting quantum computing; Sycamore; random circuits; cross-entropy benchmarking; calibration; classical simulation

## Introduction
This document supplies technical support for a quantum random-circuit-sampling experiment, rather than functioning as a standalone research article with a conventional introduction and conclusion.

## Methods
The supplement details processor design and fabrication, qubit control and readout, calibration, cross-entropy benchmarking theory, circuit construction, error analysis, and classical simulation algorithms. Full, patch, and elided circuits are compared, with computational-cost and complexity arguments.

## Results
It reports device and gate-characterization measurements, large-scale benchmarking results including 53-qubit circuits, fidelity and stability analyses, and classical simulation estimates supporting the main experiment. Performance depends on circuit structure, depth, calibration, and the error model.

## Discussion
Verification of the hardest circuits relies partly on tractable variants and modeling assumptions. Simulation cost estimates concern the specified task and algorithms; they do not establish an advantage for every computational application. The supplement includes technical qualifications and an erratum.

## Conclusion
Taken as supplementary material, it documents the methods and checks underlying the main paper's quantum-sampling claim; it does not supply an independent general-purpose computing result.
