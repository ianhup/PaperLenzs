## Title
Effects of hydrotherapy and hammock positioning on pain reduction in preterm neonates

## Topics
Neonatal pain; hydrotherapy; hammock positioning; randomized trial; non-pharmacological intervention

## Introduction
Preterm neonates experience repeated painful procedures. The study compares two non-drug interventions, separately and together, for reducing observed pain.

## Methods
Forty-five preterm neonates were randomized to hydrotherapy, hammock positioning, or their combination, with 15 per group. Daily sessions continued for 15 days. Before-and-after Neonatal Facial Coding System assessments yielded 1,350 evaluations during routine care.

## Results
Pain scores and pain prevalence decreased significantly within all three groups. The combined intervention showed the largest numerical reduction, but direct comparisons between intervention groups were not statistically significant.

## Discussion
Within-group improvement does not demonstrate superiority of one intervention, and the three active groups do not provide a usual-care-only comparison. The modest sample limits precision and the strength of comparative conclusions.

## Conclusion
The interventions appear feasible and associated with reduced pain, while evidence does not establish that combining them is superior.
