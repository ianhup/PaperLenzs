## Title
Observation of a new boson at a mass of 125 GeV with the CMS experiment at the LHC

## Topics
Particle physics; Higgs-boson search; proton collisions; decay channels; statistical significance

## Introduction
The Higgs mechanism predicts a particle associated with electroweak symmetry breaking. CMS searched for signals consistent with that particle across several decay modes.

## Methods
The study analyzed proton-proton collisions at 7 and 8 TeV, with integrated luminosities up to 5.1 and 5.3 inverse femtobarns. Five decay-channel analyses combined event reconstruction, background modeling, and statistical inference with correlated systematic uncertainties.

## Results
An excess near 125 GeV reached a local significance of five standard deviations. The high-resolution diphoton and ZZ channels yielded a mass estimate of 125.3 +/- 0.4 statistical +/- 0.5 systematic GeV. The diphoton decay excluded spin one.

## Discussion
The observed particle was consistent with a Standard Model Higgs boson, but additional measurements were needed to establish its properties. Local significance differs from significance adjusted for a search across multiple possible masses.

## Conclusion
The combined searches demonstrated a new boson near 125 GeV and motivated detailed study of its identity and interactions.
