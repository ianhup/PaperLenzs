## Title
A structural reformulation of the local entropy inequality based on the thermodynamic admissibility of exchanged energy

## Topics
Continuum thermodynamics; entropy transfer; Clausius-effective heat; nonequilibrium systems; energy balances

## Introduction
The paper questions whether every energy-transfer contribution in an internal-energy balance should also be treated as heat carrying entropy through the Clausius relation.

## Methods
A theoretical framework decomposes exchanged energy into Clausius-effective and non-Clausius contributions. The author formulates an admissibility criterion based on reversible representation, derives balance relationships, and illustrates the distinction using coupled continua and viscous heat-conducting fluids.

## Results
The proposed formulation retains all mechanisms in the energy balance but restricts entropy transfer to exchanges admitting a reversible Clausius representation. Mechanical dissipation can contribute to internal entropy production without becoming a heat-flux term.

## Discussion
The contribution is conceptual and mathematical, not an experimentally validated treatment comparison. It aims to sharpen attribution of energy pathways while preserving the first and second laws, particularly in multiphysics settings.

## Conclusion
Separating energy accounting from entropy-transfer attribution provides a proposed framework for analyzing thermodynamically different exchange mechanisms.
