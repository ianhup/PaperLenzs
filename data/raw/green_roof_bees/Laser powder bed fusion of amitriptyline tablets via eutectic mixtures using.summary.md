## Title
Laser powder bed fusion of amitriptyline tablets via eutectic mixtures using sugar alcohols as binders

## Topics
Pharmaceutical 3D printing; laser powder bed fusion; eutectic mixtures; sugar alcohols; dose calibration

## Introduction
Personalized medicine requires flexible tablet doses. This study evaluates sugar-alcohol binders and eutectic interactions as alternatives to polymer-based laser powder-bed-fusion formulations.

## Methods
Amitriptyline hydrochloride was formulated with erythritol, mannitol, and xylitol. Phase behavior, laser processing without bed preheating, dose calibration, content uniformity, mechanical strength, disintegration, and release were evaluated across dose strengths.

## Results
The calibration supported tablets in the 5-45 mg range. Mannitol and erythritol met reported content-uniformity criteria across tested doses; xylitol failed at 5 mg. Tablets had tensile strength above 1.4 MPa, disintegrated within six minutes, and released 80% of drug within ten minutes.

## Discussion
Eutectic melting-point depression reduced required energy. Attachment to the build plate limited stacking and throughput, and manufacturing quality does not establish patient-level clinical effectiveness.

## Conclusion
Sugar alcohols are promising binders for calibrated, immediate-release printed tablets, with process scalability requiring further development.
