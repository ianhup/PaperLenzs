## Title
2025 Taiwan consensus recommendations for primary care of chronic kidney disease

## Topics
Chronic kidney disease; screening; risk stratification; multidisciplinary primary care; Taiwan

## Introduction
The article addresses gaps in early detection and management of chronic kidney disease in Taiwan despite national care programs, aiming to provide practical guidance for frontline clinicians.

## Methods
Five experts reviewed international and Taiwanese guidelines and relevant research to draft recommendations. An 18-member multidisciplinary panel discussed and electronically voted on them; at least 70% agreement defined consensus and at least 90% defined strong consensus.

## Results
All ten recommendations were endorsed, nine with strong consensus. They cover risk-based screening, combined kidney-function and albuminuria assessment, staging, patient education, individualized risk-factor management, kidney-protective medications, and referral.

## Discussion
The contribution is a locally adapted consensus framework, rather than a new trial demonstrating improved patient outcomes. Its intended benefits depend on implementation, enrollment in coordinated care, and collaboration across specialties.

## Conclusion
The authors advocate standardized, coordinated primary care and timely nephrology involvement to improve identification and management of kidney disease.
